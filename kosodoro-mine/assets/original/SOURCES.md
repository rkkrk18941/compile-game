These are the original illustrations, not AI-generated approximations. All intellectual property remains with the original rights holders; this is an unofficial fan-made browser adaptation.

## Extraction

`character-*.webp`: isolate each marked Figure in rulebook page 2, preserve the PDF's ICC color space and graphics-state stack, render with transparency and rotate by the printed card's 7.75° placement angle. Hidden portions of the overlapping sample cards are retained from the separate PDF Figure groups. Artwork and Japanese printed text are unchanged.

`action-*.webp`: isolate the three normal-action card Figures from page 2, rendered at high density. `special-1..8.webp`: the eight complete original special cards on pages 12–13. `portrait-*.webp`: portrait crops of those cards for the log and result list. `ore-1/5/10.webp`: the original three denominations on page 2. `back.webp`: the printed back-design example on page 3. `cover.webp`: crop of page 1 above the rules text. `paper.webp`: blank printed-paper region on page 11. `cart.webp`: printed trolley crop from the original character card.
