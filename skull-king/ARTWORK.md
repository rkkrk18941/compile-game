# Card artwork

Skull King (Grandpa Beck’s Games edition), illustration by Apryl Stott.
Artwork © Grandpa Beck’s Games. This is an unofficial fan implementation.

The card atlas is the original product artwork displayed in the publisher-linked Board Game Arena adaptation:
https://www.grandpabecksgames.com/pages/skull-king
https://boardgamearena.com/gamepanel?game=skullking
https://x.boardgamearena.net/data/themereleases/current/games/skullking/260915-2024/img/SK_cards.jpg

Local asset: `img/cards.webp` (3472 × 1735, 14 columns × 5 rows; each card 248 × 347).
Rows: green 1–14, purple 1–14, yellow 1–14, black 1–14.
Final row: Rosie, Bendt, Rascal, Juanita, Harry, Sirena, Alyra, Skull King, Tigress, Escape, Kraken, White Whale, Loot, back.
The game preserves the English lettering in the illustrations and supplies Japanese names in the interface, accessible labels, and card reference.
